# Final card fidelity — not another ideation stage

Read request inputs: final expansion, canonical candidate, mechanism record, current cost
assessment and implementation issues. Required checks: title_anchor, claims, assumptions,
operators, evaluation_independence, resources, beginner_readability, bilingual_fidelity.

Compare ordinary English, ordinary Chinese and technical English with the SAME source.
No stronger claims, lost prerequisites, different operators, renamed evaluation objects
or independent-oracle regressions. Preserve the minimal experiment, metric/direction,
controls and failure conditions. Titles/hooks name the actual main contribution.
Certificate/verification/probe words are allowed when accurate.

Ordinary text serves readers outside this subfield: retain concept explanations,
input→operation→output and why steps matter. Explain terms on first use, avoid repeated
definitions, label pedagogical examples. Do not grade by length or research shape.
Chinese must be idiomatic and preserve claims, assumptions and resource uncertainty.

A `fail` blocks only with `executed: true` — an executed instance or a written derivation in the
evidence; a fail by judgement is recorded as unverified. Conditionals never block.

Output payload:
{"verdict":"pass | needs_work", "reason":"specific disposition",
 "checks":{"<each required check>":{"status":"pass | conditional | n_a | fail", "executed": true,
                                  "evidence":"source/output quotes or a specific defect"}}}
This does not establish efficacy or novelty. On failure preserve the draft and identify
the source-transfer error; do not invent a method.
